# MajlisAlpha Data Pack

MajlisAlpha UAE v6 loads its starter research universe, source workflow, and pilot operating data from this folder.

## Files

- companies.json: company metadata, synthetic financial metrics, thesis text, and risk-factor templates.
- documents.json: synthetic starter evidence records used by retrieval.
- questions.json: default question templates shown in the left rail.
- watchlists.json: default UAE watchlist and aliases.
- source-pack-template.json: copyable schema for adding real annual reports, earnings calls, results notes, ownership extracts, and ADX/DFM/Nasdaq Dubai disclosures.
- official-sources.json: official source map for ADX, DFM, Nasdaq Dubai CANDI, SCA, and issuer IR workflows.
- pilot-sprint.json: seven-day operating plan for making FAB, EMAAR, and ADNOCGAS pilot-ready.
- pilot-revenue.json: 14-day revenue pilot targets, ideal customer profiles, demo script, pricing hypotheses, conversion signals, and launch gate.
- catalyst-calendar.json: source-first UAE catalyst watch streams, official-source policy, calendar required fields, and readiness gate.
- peer-benchmark.json: UAE peer groups, benchmark metrics, source routes, matrix gate, and starter comparison outputs.

## Source labels

MajlisAlpha displays sourceStatus and sourceLabel in the library, dossier, and evidence cards so research output does not blur demo evidence with real source material. Keep synthetic records labelled synthetic until an analyst verifies URL, source text, period, date, and citation quality.
