# Pilot Close Room

The Pilot Close Room turns the MajlisAlpha proof packet into a paid-pilot operating decision. It exists for the moment after the founder can show value, but before the customer has clearly chosen a paid next step. At that point the product does not need more decoration; it needs a practical view of close odds, price lane, objection, evidence confidence, decision path, and the next ask.

The room reads the Pilot Proof Packet Builder, Pilot Evidence Ledger, Pilot Outreach Composer, and Pilot Conversion Pipeline. It turns those signals into a close probability, a target paid plan, weighted AED MRR, an objection label, response wording, next date, and a short close memo. The goal is to prevent vague excitement from being mistaken for revenue.

## Operating Flow

1. Run or capture a real pilot question.
2. Save proof in the Pilot Evidence Ledger.
3. Open the Pilot Proof Packet Builder and confirm the account, proof lanes, verified evidence, and next ask.
4. Open the Pilot Close Room.
5. Read the primary objection before asking for payment.
6. Use Prefill conversion to load the paid ask, probability, blocker, and close action into the Pilot Conversion Pipeline.
7. Copy the close memo for founder notes, customer follow-up, or weekly review.

## Close Signals

- Close odds combine proof-packet score, value-proof score, verified evidence, outreach motion, follow-up motion, and any active conversion probability.
- The paid ask uses AED 199, AED 399, team invoice, or discovery depending on the strength of proof and existing conversion plan.
- Objection handling separates proof gaps, trust proof, source confidence, stakeholder path, pricing confirmation, missing follow-up, and commercial next step.
- The close memo keeps the ask specific: one account, one paid lane, one objection, one next date, and one workflow.

## Use Carefully

The Close Room is commercial workflow support. It should not promise investment outcomes, portfolio returns, or advisory conclusions. If proof is weak, it should say so plainly and route the founder back to evidence, source trust, or a second real question before pushing a paid pilot.
