# Investor Objection Desk

The Investor Objection Desk is the v61 workflow layer after the Investor Update Composer. It is designed for the honest investor conversation: the founder has a credible update, but the next useful work is answering the skeptical questions without hiding proof gaps.

The desk converts the live investor update, data-room package, momentum ledger, revenue forecast, account health, founder diligence, source coverage, and deployment controls into six answer lanes: category, demand, revenue, controls, proof gap, and ask. Each lane produces a short reply, the proof behind that reply, the missing evidence, the owner, and the next workflow target.

The score intentionally stays conservative. A high objection score requires more than polished language: it needs verified evidence, source rows, customer or relationship signal, revenue motion, control readiness, and a specific next ask. When the score is low, the panel still helps by showing the exact objection that deserves the next founder hour.
