# Investor Meeting Prep Room

The Investor Meeting Prep Room is the v61 workflow layer that sits after the Investor Reply Pipeline. It turns a relationship signal into a meeting operating plan: opening context, demo route, proof stack, objection prep, and the exact next ask.

## What It Checks

- Relationship signal from warm replies, open deals, follow-ups, and saved outreach drafts.
- Proof stack from the Investor Data Room, Pilot Proof Packet, source rows, and current cited answer context.
- Agenda clarity from the Investor Intro Room and the reply pipeline.
- Objection prep from the Founder Diligence Room, review log, and decision journal.
- Exact ask from weighted MRR, conversion records, warm replies, and ask-quality score.

## How To Use It

Open the room before an investor, advisor, or partner conversation. The six prep cards show whether the meeting is ready or still missing proof. The agenda rows tell the founder what to open next: reply signal, intro route, proof packet, data room, diligence, or conversion pipeline.

Use **Copy meeting brief** before the call to create a lightweight founder prep note. Use **Export meeting JSON** when the prep should be stored as an operating artifact for a release handoff, founder review, or board pack.
