# MajlisAlpha Answer Quality Lab

Answer Quality Lab gives MajlisAlpha a repeatable evaluation layer. It checks whether a generated answer is source-backed, UAE-specific, traceable, logical, useful, and ready for a pilot user.

## Why It Matters

The desk now has source intake, claim trace, disclosure alerts, portfolio radar, peer benchmarking, and mission control. The quality lab ties those layers together by asking one blunt question: should this answer leave the desk?

## Score Bands

Use four score bands:

- Publish-ready: 85 or higher.
- Review-ready: 70 to 84.
- Source-blocked: 50 to 69.
- Reject: below 50.

If source coverage or claim trace fails, the answer cannot be publish-ready regardless of total score.

## Evaluation Dimensions

Score answers across six dimensions:

- Source coverage.
- Claim trace.
- UAE market specificity.
- Valuation and logic.
- User value.
- Review readiness.

The two most important checks are source coverage and claim trace. A polished answer with weak evidence should stay internal.

## Failure Reasons

Common failure reasons include:

- Missing official source route.
- Unsupported material claim.
- Stale period or unclear date.
- Generic non-UAE answer.
- Valuation math does not tie out.
- No next action.
- Reviewer status missing.

Failure reasons should create Mission Control tasks rather than disappear into notes.

## Launch Gate

The lab is pilot-ready when exported answers can show score band, dimension scores, failure reasons, reviewer status, and next action. If the answer cannot explain why it passed, it should not be exported.
