# Funding Round Command Center

The Funding Round Command Center is the v61 layer after the Investor Decision Room. It turns investor decisions, proof packets, revenue signal, stakeholder warmth, risk controls, and follow-up dates into one operating view for a fundraising, strategic-pilot, or partner-capital motion.

## What It Measures

- Round readiness from the decision room, IC memo, diligence proof, commercial case, and risk controls.
- Investor pipeline from relationships, warm replies, outreach drafts, follow-ups, and conversion records.
- Diligence proof from source evidence, REAL/imported citations, data-room readiness, and IC memo strength.
- Commercial case from weighted MRR, forecast MRR, run-rate ARR, and advanced conversion signal.
- Risk controls from objection handling, Pages Doctor health, review notes, and non-advisory boundaries.
- Closing motion from dated follow-ups, saved decision notes, pilot sessions, and warm reply activity.

## How To Use

1. Open Investor Decision Room and confirm the next decision gap.
2. Open Funding Round Command Center.
3. Review the six command cards and six round lanes.
4. Use `Open next round gap` to jump to the highest-priority workflow.
5. Copy the round command or export JSON before investor follow-up.
