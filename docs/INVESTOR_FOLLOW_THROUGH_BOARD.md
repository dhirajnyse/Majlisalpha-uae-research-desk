# Investor Follow-Through Board

The Investor Follow-Through Board is the v61 workflow layer after the Investor Meeting Prep Room. It exists for the founder moment after a useful conversation, when the most important question is no longer how to prepare, but how to keep momentum from leaking.

## What It Checks

- Recap discipline from saved outreach notes, open follow-ups, and due follow-through items.
- Proof delivery from the meeting proof stack, Pilot Proof Packet, verified evidence, and source rows.
- Intro asks from warm replies, open conversion paths, ask quality, and weighted MRR.
- Decision path from conversion records, open deal routes, warm replies, and commercial signal.
- Next-date hygiene from dated follow-ups, conversion next dates, and overdue items.

## How To Use It

Open the board after an investor, advisor, partner, or champion meeting. Start with the score, then work the relationship rows in order: send the recap, deliver the strongest proof packet, ask for one named operator or sponsor, convert interest into a tracked path, and set the next date.

Use **Copy follow-through note** when preparing a founder update or outbound recap. Use **Export follow-through JSON** when the relationship state should be stored as a structured artifact for the release handoff, founder review, board pack, or conversion pipeline.
