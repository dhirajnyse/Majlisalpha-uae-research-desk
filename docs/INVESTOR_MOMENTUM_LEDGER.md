# Investor Momentum Ledger

The Investor Momentum Ledger is the v61 workflow layer after the Investor Follow-Through Board. It helps the founder decide which relationship deserves the next founder hour when several investor, advisor, partner, or customer conversations are moving at once.

The ledger blends follow-through score, proof delivery, proof-request blockers, warm replies, conversion value, open follow-ups, due dates, and saved outreach into one momentum score. It is intentionally practical: the board should answer, "Who should I act on next, what proof do they need, and what is the next 48-hour move?"

The live surface contains six cards: momentum score, priority relationships, proof requests, warm intros, conversion value, and next 48 hours. It also contains five ledger rows for relationship priority, proof request, intro ask, commercial path, and dated next action. Each row routes back to the workflow that can fix the gap.

Use the primary action to open the next momentum move. Use Open follow-through to return to the post-meeting queue. Copy momentum ledger creates a founder-ready text note, while Export momentum JSON saves the underlying relationship signals for review.
