# Founder Board Pack Center

The Founder Board Pack Center turns MajlisAlpha operating signals into a weekly update that can be shared with a founder, advisor, pilot stakeholder, or early investor. It combines the Founder Revenue Forecast, Account Health Command Center, Pilot Proof Packet, Pilot Close Room, Renewal & Expansion Board, Pages Doctor, and Live Smoke Test into one practical memo.

The goal is not to make the product look bigger than it is. The goal is to tell the truth crisply: what revenue exists, what pipeline is forming, which account needs attention, what evidence supports the commercial ask, whether the deployed site is healthy, and what one action should happen this week.

Use the board pack before sharing a build or asking for a paid-pilot decision. A strong pack should show a named revenue story, customer proof, account health, close motion, retention motion, deploy confidence, and one explicit founder ask. If the pack is weak, it should still be useful by routing the operator to the next gap: evidence ledger, proof packet, account health, revenue forecast, conversion pipeline, renewal board, or Pages Doctor.

Post-upload test:

1. Open Founder Board Pack Center from the hero.
2. Confirm the board cards show readiness, revenue story, account health, proof packet, deployment health, and next founder ask.
3. Confirm the board sections route to revenue forecast, proof packet, account health, close/conversion, renewal, deployment, and evidence workflows.
4. Use `Copy board memo` or `Export board JSON` before a founder review, pilot review, or live-site handoff.
