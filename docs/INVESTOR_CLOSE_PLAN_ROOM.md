# Investor Close Plan Room

The Investor Close Plan Room is the v61 founder workflow for turning investor commitment motion into the next signed action. It sits after the Investor Commitment Tracker and scores six close lanes: decision owner, proof package, commercial ask, intro close, calendar lock, and risk controls.

Use the room when an investor, advisor, operator, or source partner is interested but the next action is still vague. The room combines commitment readiness, proof requests, verified evidence, source rows, conversion records, warm replies, follow-ups, objection clearance, and deployment health into one close plan.

The primary output is not hype. It is a copyable and exportable close plan that says what to ask for next: data-room review, named operator intro, paid-pilot decision date, source partnership, or funding conversation. It also shows the weakest lane so the founder can fix proof, calendar, commercial, or control gaps before asking for a stronger commitment.
