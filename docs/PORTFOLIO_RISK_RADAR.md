# MajlisAlpha Portfolio Risk Radar

Portfolio Risk Radar turns MajlisAlpha from a company research desk into a portfolio triage workflow. It helps analysts decide which UAE exposure deserves attention first based on concentration, evidence freshness, catalyst pressure, and required next action.

## What It Solves

Single-company research can look clean while the portfolio still carries hidden concentration. A user may hold multiple names exposed to the same rate, property-cycle, dividend, governance, or liquidity driver. The radar makes those shared risks visible before the next memo or committee review.

## Exposure Buckets

The radar starts with four buckets:

- Financials: FAB, ENBD, and DIB.
- Property and development: EMAAR and ALDAR.
- Energy and utilities: ADNOCGAS and DEWA.
- Platforms, telecom, and travel: IHC, EAND, and AIRARABIA.

Each bucket has its own watch drivers and a radar question. The question is meant to trigger research work, not to produce an automatic investment call.

## Signal Rules

A portfolio alert is actionable only when these fields exist together:

- Exposure bucket.
- Ticker or tickers.
- Source status.
- Catalyst context.
- Risk driver.
- Next analyst action.
- Decision-journal status.

If the evidence is stale, synthetic, or unreviewed, the radar should label the item as source-blocked.

## Daily Workflow

1. Review the top watchlist buckets.
2. Check whether any bucket has stale or missing evidence.
3. Cross-check catalyst pressure from the Catalyst Calendar.
4. Use the Peer Benchmark Matrix before increasing exposure.
5. Create a risk memo when several holdings share one driver.
6. Record hold, review, trim, or watch in the Decision Journal.

## Launch Gate

The radar is pilot-ready when each watchlist bucket has a current source status, catalyst status, concentration note, and next action. If the portfolio view cannot explain why a holding is green, yellow, red, or source-blocked, keep it in demo mode.
