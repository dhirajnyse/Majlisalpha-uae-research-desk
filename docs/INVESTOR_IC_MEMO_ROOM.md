# Investor IC Memo Room

The Investor IC Memo Room packages the investor workflow into a committee-style operating memo. It sits after the Investor Terms & Follow-Up Room and turns the current path into a concise decision pack for a founder, advisor, or investor committee review.

## What It Checks

- Memo readiness across narrative, source proof, commercial ask, risk controls, decision path, and follow-up motion.
- Investment question clarity from the Founder Diligence Room, Founder Board Pack Center, Investor Data Room, and terms path.
- Proof pack depth from current citations, REAL or imported sources, source-pack records, and Pilot Evidence Ledger entries.
- Commercial ask quality from weighted MRR, forecast MRR, conversion records, revenue forecast, and the close plan.
- Risk controls from the Investor Objection Desk, compliance boundary, deployment health, and non-advisory language.
- Decision path quality from warm replies, relationships, saved decisions, and dated follow-ups.

## Main Outputs

- A readiness hero showing whether the IC memo is ready, forming, or missing key proof.
- Six cards for memo readiness, investment question, proof pack, commercial ask, risk controls, and decision path.
- Six memo sections covering thesis, source proof, commercial case, risks, decision owner, and next action.
- One next-gap button that opens the workflow panel most likely to improve the memo.
- Copy and JSON export actions for handoff, review, and founder operating notes.

## Recommended Use

1. Run or import at least one UAE-market question.
2. Add official or imported source evidence where possible.
3. Open Investor Terms & Follow-Up Room and confirm the action type, owner, proof bundle, and follow-up date.
4. Open Investor IC Memo Room and review the next memo gap.
5. Copy the memo for discussion, or export JSON for a tracked operating archive.
