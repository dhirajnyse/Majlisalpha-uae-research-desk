# Founder Diligence Room

The Founder Diligence Room turns the MajlisAlpha operating stack into answers for the hard questions a founder, advisor, pilot stakeholder, or early investor will ask before taking the UAE product seriously. It pulls from the Founder Board Pack, Founder Revenue Forecast, Account Health Command Center, Pilot Proof Packet, Pages Doctor, and Live Smoke Test so the discussion stays grounded in commercial proof, customer proof, source quality, controls, and deployment health.

Use it when the product looks promising but the next room will ask sharper questions: why UAE first, who is paying or likely to pay, what evidence is real versus projected, whether the official-source workflow is defensible, what could break trust, and what the single next proof gap is. The room should not overstate traction. A weak answer is still useful if it names the next operating move clearly.

The best diligence memo has seven parts:

1. Market wedge: the specific UAE disclosure workflow MajlisAlpha owns first.
2. Customer proof: real sessions, repeat questions, saved outputs, follow-ups, and named account health.
3. Revenue proof: current AED MRR, expected close pipeline, renewal/expansion signal, and at-risk revenue.
4. Source moat: official ADX, DFM, Nasdaq Dubai, issuer IR, and imported-document evidence discipline.
5. Controls: non-advisory framing, review status, citation quality, export safety, and deployment confidence.
6. Gaps: source, commercial, proof, workflow, or trust blockers that still prevent a paid pilot ask.
7. Next action: one dated move that improves the evidence story before the next founder review.

Post-upload test:

1. Open Founder Diligence Room from the hero.
2. Confirm it shows diligence readiness, commercial proof, customer proof, source moat, risk controls, and next hard question cards.
3. Use `Open next diligence gap` and confirm it routes to the most important missing proof section.
4. Use `Copy diligence memo` or `Export diligence JSON` before a founder/advisor review.
