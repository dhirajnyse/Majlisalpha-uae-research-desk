# Investor Data Room

The Investor Data Room packages the MajlisAlpha founder story into a shareable review packet for an advisor, serious pilot stakeholder, early investor, or partner who wants more than a product screenshot. It pulls together the Founder Board Pack, Founder Diligence Room, Founder Revenue Forecast, Account Health Command Center, Pilot Proof Packet, source provenance, Pages Doctor, and Live Smoke Test.

The room is not meant to make MajlisAlpha look artificially mature. It should make the current truth easy to inspect: what the UAE wedge is, what proof exists, what revenue is real or forecast, which customer evidence is still missing, how the official-source workflow is defended, what controls protect trust, and what one next ask should be made.

Use the Investor Data Room before sending a link to someone who may influence funding, introductions, paid-pilot conversion, or product direction. If the score is weak, do not hide it. Use the gap routing to improve the most important missing piece before sharing externally.

The review packet should contain:

1. Narrative: one clean founder story anchored in the UAE source-first wedge.
2. Diligence: answers to the hard questions about market, customer, revenue, proof, defensibility, and controls.
3. Revenue: forecast MRR, weighted MRR, ARR run-rate, expansion upside, and at-risk revenue.
4. Customer evidence: use cases, source proof, output proof, workflow proof, and revenue proof captured in the evidence ledger.
5. Source spine: official-source records, imported filings, source status, and provenance discipline.
6. Controls: deployment health, review trail, decision trail, non-advisory framing, and export safety.
7. Ask: one specific milestone, introduction, paid-pilot request, source contribution, or founder action.

Post-upload test:

1. Open Investor Data Room from the hero.
2. Confirm it shows room readiness, narrative spine, commercial folder, customer evidence, source and controls, and next share gap cards.
3. Confirm the share package lists narrative, diligence, revenue, customer proof, source spine, controls, and ask.
4. Use `Open next data-room gap` to route to the weakest packet item.
5. Use `Copy data-room memo` or `Export data-room JSON` before external founder/advisor review.
