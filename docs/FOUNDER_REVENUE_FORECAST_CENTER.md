# Founder Revenue Forecast Center

The Founder Revenue Forecast Center turns MajlisAlpha pilot evidence into a practical revenue view. It combines Account Health, Renewal & Expansion, Pilot Conversion Pipeline, paid-intent sessions, and revenue-proof evidence entries into forecast MRR, annualized ARR, expansion upside, and MRR at risk.

Use this center when the founder needs to decide what to push this week. The section shows committed MRR from paid-pilot or won records, weighted open pipeline, expansion-ready account upside, annualized run-rate, proof backing, and rescue exposure. Its scenario cards then route the next action to the correct workflow: protect weak MRR, convert open pipeline, test expansion, attach revenue proof, name the ARR story, or improve account-health discipline.

Operating rule: never present the forecast as final revenue unless the supporting workflow evidence is visible. A clean forecast should have a priced conversion record, a named next close action, at least one paid-intent or revenue-proof signal, and account-health/renewal scores that explain why the number is credible.

Post-upload test:

1. Open Founder Revenue Forecast Center from the hero.
2. Confirm the forecast cards show committed MRR, weighted pipeline, expansion upside, ARR run-rate, revenue proof, and MRR at risk.
3. Click `Prefill conversion` and confirm the Pilot Conversion Pipeline receives the next account, plan, MRR, probability, blocker, and close action.
4. Copy or export the revenue memo before a founder or pilot-account review.
