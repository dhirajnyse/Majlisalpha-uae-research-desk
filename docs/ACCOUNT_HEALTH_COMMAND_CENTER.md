# Account Health Command Center

The Account Health Command Center turns MajlisAlpha from a single-demo workflow into a founder operating board for multiple UAE pilot accounts. It reads Pilot Sessions, Pilot Follow-Up Board, Pilot Outreach Composer, Pilot Conversion Pipeline, Pilot Evidence Ledger, Paid Pilot Delivery Board, and Renewal & Expansion Board to score each account across activation, trust, retention, revenue, and motion.

Use this board when there is more than one user, lead, customer, or paid-pilot account in motion. Instead of asking â€œwhat feature should we build next?â€ the board asks a sharper operating question: which account is weakest, what signal is missing, and where should the founder act next?

The scoring model is practical rather than theoretical:

- Activation comes from captured sessions, real questions, and second-question behavior.
- Trust comes from REAL source sessions, source-proof evidence, verified evidence, and output proof.
- Retention comes from repeat evidence, second questions, and active follow-up motion.
- Revenue comes from paid-intent sessions, conversion pipeline MRR, revenue-proof evidence, and paid/won stages.
- Motion comes from dated follow-ups, outreach, conversion records, and evidence depth.

The board sorts accounts from weakest to strongest so the next action is obvious. A rescue account routes back to pilot session, evidence ledger, or follow-up. A watch account routes to retention or revenue work. A healthy account routes toward Renewal & Expansion so the next commercial ask is grounded in proof.

Use the follow-up prefill button to move the weakest account into a concrete next action. This keeps the operating cadence tight: every account should have a named owner, source/trust posture, next date, and commercial lane before the founder treats it as healthy.
