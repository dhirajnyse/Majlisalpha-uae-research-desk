# Investor Decision Room

The Investor Decision Room is the v61 workflow layer after the Investor IC Memo Room. It turns committee-style feedback into an explicit next path: approve pilot, request proof, ask for an introduction, continue diligence, wait or no-go, or set the next review date.

## What It Measures

- Decision readiness from the IC memo, owner clarity, review trail, and saved decision notes.
- Proof request readiness from source count, REAL/imported citations, evidence ledger items, and data-room health.
- Commercial decision quality from weighted MRR, forecast MRR, conversion records, and close-plan commercial signal.
- Risk gate from objection handling, non-advisory boundaries, close-plan risk controls, and deployment health.
- Stakeholder signal from relationships, warm replies, outreach drafts, and decision notes.
- Follow-up clock from sessions, follow-ups, open follow-up count, and decision trail.

## How To Use

1. Open Investor IC Memo Room and review the memo gap.
2. Open Investor Decision Room.
3. Read the six decision cards and the six decision outcomes.
4. Use `Open next decision gap` to jump to the highest-priority next workflow.
5. Copy the decision room or export JSON before sending an investor follow-up.
