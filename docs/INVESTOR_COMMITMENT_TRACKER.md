# Investor Commitment Tracker

The Investor Commitment Tracker is the v61 workflow layer after the Investor Objection Desk. It is built for the founder moment when interest sounds positive, objections have been answered, and the next mistake would be leaving the relationship as a vague warm thread.

The tracker converts answered objections, investor warmth, proof requests, commercial signal, intro leverage, and follow-up discipline into six commitment paths: data-room review, proof request, warm intro, commercial decision, dated follow-up, and update loop. Each path names the ask, owner, signal, missing proof, next target section, and whether the commitment is ready or still needs evidence.

The score is intentionally operational rather than promotional. It rewards named relationships, warm replies, proof that can be sent, revenue or paid-pilot motion, clear intro leverage, dated next steps, and objection clearance. When the score is low, the tracker still gives the founder a useful next move instead of pretending the raise or partnership conversation is ready.
