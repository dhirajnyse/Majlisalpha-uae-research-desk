# Renewal & Expansion Board

The Renewal & Expansion Board turns paid-pilot delivery into the next commercial decision. It reads the Paid Pilot Delivery Board, Pilot Close Room, Pilot Conversion Pipeline, Pilot Follow-Up Board, and Pilot Evidence Ledger to decide whether the account is ready for renewal, team expansion, or a rescue move before the pilot loses momentum.

Use the board after the first paid week is operating. A strong renewal motion needs a clear scope, paid lane, first paid brief, review cadence, repeated value signal, and at least one credible source-backed artifact. If those signals are weak, the board names the missing proof and routes the operator back to delivery, follow-up, evidence, or close-room work.

The board is intentionally practical:

- Renewal signal checks whether delivery and follow-up behavior support keeping access active.
- Expansion signal checks whether the account has enough usage, revenue, or team-access intent to justify a larger plan.
- Churn risk highlights whether the account is still high-risk because there is no won stage, no dated review, no repeat evidence, or no output artifact.
- Expansion MRR estimates the next commercial lane using conversion records and pilot intent.
- Plays convert the score into actions: secure a renewal date, package a team-access ask, rescue weak proof, or copy a follow-up plan.

Run the renewal board before asking for a bigger commitment. It is a founder operating view, not investment advice, and it should stay attached to source proof, user behavior, and dated next actions.
