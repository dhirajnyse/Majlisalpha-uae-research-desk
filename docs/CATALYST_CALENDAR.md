# MajlisAlpha UAE Catalyst Calendar

The Catalyst Calendar turns MajlisAlpha from a research desk into a recurring market workflow. It helps users know what to watch next, but it stays source-first: no catalyst date should be shown as real unless it is backed by an official exchange, issuer, regulator, or government URL.

## What The Calendar Tracks

The calendar is focused on watch streams rather than invented event dates:

- Bank results and rates: FAB, ENBD, and DIB.
- Property cycle and presales: EMAAR and ALDAR.
- Energy, utilities, and dividends: ADNOCGAS and DEWA.
- Market notices and corporate actions: IHC, EAND, AIRARABIA, and broader ADX/DFM/Nasdaq Dubai notices.

Each stream gives the analyst a short list of signals to monitor and the official routes where those signals should be verified.

## Source Policy

A calendar item is not REAL until the analyst captures:

- Issuer and ticker.
- Event type.
- Official URL.
- Source date.
- Market date.
- Analyst action.
- Review status.

Expected windows can exist as watch items, but they should not be displayed as confirmed events. This protects the product from accidentally selling guesses as research.

## Daily Workflow

Morning:

- Check ADX and DFM disclosure pages.
- Review issuer IR pages for priority names.
- Scan Nasdaq Dubai CANDI where relevant.
- Move any new official item into the source queue.

Research session:

- Attach each event to a question template.
- Run the Source Intake Doctor.
- Create a memo packet if the event changes risk, valuation, or management-tone view.

End of day:

- Mark events as watch, REAL, reviewed, or blocked.
- Add decision-journal notes for items that changed the thesis.
- Keep unverified expected windows separate from official dated events.

## Launch Gate

The calendar is launch-ready when at least three catalyst streams have REAL source-backed items, each linked to a memo, company dossier, or decision-journal entry. If the calendar is only a list of expected dates, it is not ready.
