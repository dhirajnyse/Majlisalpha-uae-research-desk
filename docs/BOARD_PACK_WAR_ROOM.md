# Board Pack War Room

The Board Pack War Room is the v61 layer after the Funding Round Command Center. It turns round command, investor IC memo, diligence proof, revenue forecast, risk controls, and follow-up motion into a board-ready pack with one decision, one owner, and one next date.

## What It Measures

- Pack readiness across narrative, evidence, finance, decision agenda, controls, and round command.
- Executive narrative strength from round readiness, founder board pack, IC memo, reviews, and decision notes.
- Evidence appendix depth from verified evidence, REAL citations, imported citations, and investor data-room readiness.
- Finance bridge quality from forecast MRR, weighted pipeline, run-rate ARR, paid-intent sessions, and conversion records.
- Decision agenda clarity from investor pipeline, warm replies, relationships, open follow-ups, and board-account context.
- Control readiness from risk controls, governance score, Pages Doctor, Smoke Test, reviews, and decision trail.

## How To Use It

1. Run one representative UAE research question.
2. Open Funding Round Command Center and confirm the round motion.
3. Open Board Pack War Room.
4. Use the next board gap button to jump to the weakest required workflow.
5. Copy the board pack when preparing a founder update, board discussion, investor committee prep, or partner-capital conversation.
6. Export board JSON when preserving the pack state for a release handoff or review trail.
