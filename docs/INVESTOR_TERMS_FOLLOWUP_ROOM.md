# Investor Terms & Follow-Up Room

The Investor Terms & Follow-Up Room is the v61 workflow after the Investor Close Plan Room. It is built for the moment when the founder has a close plan but still needs to convert it into a concrete investor operating path with action type, owner, date, proof bundle, commercial terms, stakeholder route, and guardrails.

The room scores six practical lanes: terms readiness, owner clarity, commercial terms, proof lock, follow-up date, and boundary control. It uses the close plan, commitment tracker, investor momentum ledger, founder update, revenue forecast, account health, evidence ledger, and deployment controls to identify the weakest next-step gap.

Use it before sending a serious follow-up to an investor, advisor, operator, or source partner. The goal is to avoid vague momentum. The output should answer what lane is being advanced, who owns the next yes or unlock, what proof is attached, what commercial option is being discussed, when the next decision happens, and how the message stays non-advisory and auditable.
