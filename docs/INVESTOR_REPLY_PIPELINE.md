# Investor Reply Pipeline

The Investor Reply Pipeline turns the MajlisAlpha Investor Intro Room into an operating relationship queue. It is meant for the founder moment after a data room or intro draft exists, when the important question becomes: who deserves follow-up now, what proof should be attached, and which reply should move into conversion or source review.

## What It Checks

- Pipeline readiness across intro quality, outbound draft count, follow-up activity, warm replies, conversion records, and weighted MRR.
- Intro assets from the Advisor, Investor, Partner, and Follow-up drafts.
- Warm replies from conversion records and saved outbound drafts.
- Open relationships across follow-up and conversion workflows.
- Due follow-ups, so a real relationship does not get buried under new outreach.
- Next reply move, with a routed button into the workflow that needs attention first.

## How To Use It

Open Investor Reply Pipeline after preparing the Investor Intro Room. Start with the readiness score, then inspect the six cards and four relationship rows. If a row is weak, use the primary next-move button to jump into the exact source workflow: outreach composer, follow-up board, conversion pipeline, evidence ledger, account health, or intro room. Copy the pipeline for a founder operating note, or export JSON for a structured handoff.
