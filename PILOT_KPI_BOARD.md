# MajlisAlpha Macro & Rates Radar

Macro & Rates Radar connects company answers to the UAE macro tape. It helps MajlisAlpha explain whether an issuer signal is being shaped by rates, oil, the AED/USD peg context, real estate liquidity, bank funding, inflation pressure, or government spending.

## Purpose

Company evidence is strongest when the desk also understands the market driver behind it. A bank margin comment, property cash-conversion trend, energy pipeline update, utility funding plan, or airline demand signal can mean different things depending on the rate cycle, dollar backdrop, oil regime, and liquidity conditions.

The radar records each macro driver as an auditable item:

- driver type
- source route
- official URL
- observation date
- affected sectors
- affected tickers
- assumption impact
- evidence snippet
- owner
- reviewer status
- next action

## Driver Types

- Rates and funding: policy-rate pressure, deposit costs, net interest margin, mortgage demand, refinancing risk, and discount-rate assumptions.
- Oil and fiscal spending: oil regime, government spending, project awards, infrastructure activity, and confidence indicators.
- AED/USD peg and dollar strength: imported inflation, dollar funding, tourism flows, cross-border exposure, and valuation assumptions.
- Real estate liquidity: off-plan sales, handovers, collections, mortgage pressure, rental demand, and developer cash conversion.
- Inflation and operating costs: wage inflation, input costs, energy costs, pricing power, margin bridge, and scenario sensitivity.

## Workflow

1. Capture the macro driver from an official or issuer-backed source.
2. Classify driver type and observation date.
3. Map affected sectors and tickers.
4. Record valuation, margin, funding, liquidity, or risk impact.
5. Route material drivers to Peer Benchmark Matrix, Portfolio Risk Radar, Answer Quality Lab, Catalyst Calendar, or Claim Trace Inspector.
6. Mark the macro read-through usable only after reviewer approval.

## Escalation Rules

- Rates or funding changes that affect banks, property, or discount-rate language go to Answer Quality Lab and Peer Benchmark Matrix.
- Oil, fiscal, or project-spending drivers that affect sector outlook go to Catalyst Calendar and Portfolio Risk Radar.
- Unsupported macro drivers with missing URL, date, or evidence snippet route back to Source Refresh Scheduler.
- Macro drivers that change customer-facing claims or valuation scenarios require Claim Trace Inspector review before export.

## Launch Gate

Macro & Rates Radar is pilot-ready when every material macro driver has driver type, source route, official URL, observation date, affected tickers, assumption impact, owner, and reviewer status.

It is not ready when the desk cannot prove the driver source, date, affected ticker mapping, assumption impact, and reviewer decision.
